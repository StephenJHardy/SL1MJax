# Review of the diagonal survey — 9 September 2026

## Overall assessment

The work delivers a useful **centre-frequency survey and limited imaging
adapter**. It does not yet satisfy every gate in the original band-wide plan.
The main advance is a named physical EVLA-C model, compared directly with both
executions, with an explicit supported-frequency policy and a real seven-pointing
smoke test. Full-Jones acceptance is correctly kept separate.

The evidence supports an opt-in Stokes-I imaging experiment in the 3C391 band.
It does not establish a continuously validated 4–8 GHz beam, precise sidelobe
zeros, or a physical beam failure wherever calibrated visibility power is poor.

## Findings

### 1. The published first-null interval can identify a later minimum

`src/sl1mjax/evla_c_survey_plots.py:first_null_from_radial` searches medians in
10-arcminute annuli. At 4.564 GHz, the published interval is **20–30 arcmin**.
Read-only evaluation of the actual survey plane gives its first positive-axis
magnitude minima at:

| Cut | RR | LL |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| l axis | 10.60′ | 10.47′ |
| m axis | 10.79′ | 10.29′ |

The next model minima lie around 22–23′. At 7.948 GHz the first minima are
5.98–6.27′, smaller than one diagnostic radial bin. These model-cut positions
are interpolation diagnostics, not precise measured null estimates.

Calling the 20–30′ annulus a sampling-limited **first** null is therefore unsafe.
It is a minimum of a coarse radial statistic, not evidence that the first null
lies there. This matters directly for bright out-of-field source predictions.

Required change: rename the existing quantity as a coarse radial minimum. Add
model-resolved first-minimum locations per azimuth and compare the actual dense
measured samples around those locations. Mark the measured first null unresolved
where those samples cannot bracket it. Test a beam with two nearby minima to
show that coarse rebinning cannot silently relabel the second as the first.

### 2. Frequency scores lack the common-population control specified in the plan

`evla_c_survey_compare.load_holoraster_slot` builds regions from measured response.
`score_extract` uses those regions without a second fixed-geometry/model mask.
This preserves the historical metric, but contaminated amplitude can affect
region membership. Channel flags also change the population between scores.

`write_slot_products` saves measured/predicted/weight and offsets, but omits
original row IDs, time, scan, antenna IDs, per-row source model, and partition
identities. The compact exports cannot establish common-row frequency scores or
locate anomalous baselines without reopening the MS. The plan explicitly
requested these fields and the second mask.

Required change: add a versioned row-provenance sidecar or richer export. Keep
the existing scores, but add a shared fixed-region mask, matched-row frequency
scores, and outlier influence by scan/antenna. Do not discard outliers or change
flags based on these diagnostics. A large residual-power score is not by itself
evidence of a frequency-local beam defect.

### 3. Completion and numerical qualification are overstated

The slot ledger has 44 scored slots out of 160. The other 116 are `pass_a_only`.
The implementation assigns that state to unscored Pass-B slots once the centre
survey completes. This is a scope reduction, not execution of their declared
within-SPW tests. It is acceptable to stop there, but the final acceptance must
say **Pass A complete; Pass B limited to lower-C SPWs 4–6**.

The machine-readable `status.json` still has `phase3_convergence: pending`,
several finished stages labelled running/pending, and `outcome: null`.
The current classifier calls a slot scientifically qualified from the two
main-lobe residual-power thresholds alone. It does not consult a per-frequency
numerical-convergence result or a calibration-validation result.

Required change: reconcile the durable ledger with actual products. Supply the
planned low/mid/high aperture-resolution and angular-sampling convergence
evidence, or explicitly retain numerical qualification as incomplete. Preserve
"passes the empirical residual cut" separately from "numerically qualified".
Do not inherit a single-frequency refresh result as band-wide convergence.

### 4. Upper-C archive integrity is unresolved

The inventory and handoff record an inner `ms.tgz` CRC error. Opening casacore
tables establishes readability, not integrity of the visibility payload.
The recorded integrity problem does not prove any particular channel is bad,
but it prevents treating upper-C discrepancies as clean beam evidence.

Required change: locate the damaged archive member/data range, compare with an
independent archive checksum or replacement extraction, or attach an explicit
integrity-unverified qualification to every upper-C scientific claim. Preserve
the current diagnostic results; do not overwrite the archive or work products.

## Smaller contract risks

- The shared channel reader hard-codes circular correlation order, ignores
  `FLAG_ROW`, and always broadcasts `WEIGHT` instead of testing per-row
  `WEIGHT_SPECTRUM` contents. These were justified for an early product, not
  automatically for each new MS. A real SPW-4 check found 129,685 FLAG_ROW rows
  and **zero** cases where channel-32 flags failed to duplicate them. Thus that
  omission does not invalidate the SPW-4 background test, but the reader still
  needs general contract tests before reuse.
- The handoff's continuous-looking 4436–5820 MHz range is a collection of tested
  centres, not continuous channel acceptance. SPW-4 4548/4580 MHz already have
  hand-specific misses inside that range.
- The native smoke used 32 rows per pointing and three frequencies. Its matching
  train/reload loss (0.0380277) is a genuine engineering check, not convergence or
  validation of a final mosaic. The exact-frequency adapter still needs the
  remaining requested native planes before a full 64-channel run.
- An idle old Bacchus wrapper was still present during review. No survey compute
  process was active. The wrapper was not stopped or modified.

## Recommended route back to imaging

1. Correct the null labels and close the status/provenance qualifications.
2. Retain the unit EVLA-C diagonal model as an opt-in physics prior; do not tune
   beam width, squint, or leakage from the survey's unexplained channel spikes.
3. Generate exact missing native 3C391 planes, or explicitly start with a small
   fixed channel subset. Keep frequency interpolation refused until tested.
4. Run one shared-sky seven-pointing reconstruction with unchanged FOV, guard,
   catalogue and folds. Record numerical integration and optimization error;
   do not present a fixed-step beam ranking as a converged comparison.
5. Keep bright out-of-field source terms and report their uncertainty. The
   present survey does not yet validate outer-lobe amplitude or null alignment
   to the accuracy needed for strong claims about those sources.

## Verification performed in this review

- Inspected the plan, live and machine-readable ledgers, calibration policies,
  channel reports, generated-model lookup, plotting, scorer/export and smoke code.
- Queried Bacchus process state and inspected the SPW-4 flag/pointing metadata
  read-only. No survey product or running process was changed.
- Focused survey/adapter/background/independent-RIME tests: **52 passed** with
  `MPLBACKEND=Agg`. The first invocation aborted in the macOS GUI plotting backend;
  the headless rerun passed. This is not a claim that the full repository passed.
- Added and tested the background snapshot's spherical repointing check separately.
  The real-data background diagnostic is recorded in its own product directory
  and companion report, not merged into survey acceptance.
- Broader final run: **123 passed** in 198 seconds, covering the above plus
  voltage reconstruction, bundle loading, holography coordinates, explicit JAX
  adjoint, and forward closure. This is still a focused selection, not the full
  repository suite.
- The real background test is complete. See
  [BACKGROUND_TEST_20260909.md](BACKGROUND_TEST_20260909.md). Its fixed α=−0.7
  catalogue prediction is 0.76/0.97 mJy RR/LL RMS against 481/477 mJy residual
  RMS on 1,240 supported rows. It does not explain the dominant floor there;
  unsupported wider-raster rows remain untested.
